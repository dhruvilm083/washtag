Two ways to scan

Symbols on the label, or the code on the box.

Clothes carry care symbols. Detergents, softeners and stain removers carry barcodes. Washtag reads both, and when a label has symbols and a barcode side by side it reads them together.

Care symbols

Photograph a wash label flat and in focus. Each symbol is decoded into a temperature, a cycle and a list of things to avoid, with a confidence score next to every one. Text in other languages is translated.

wash ≤30°Cno bleachno tumbleiron ·dry clean P

UPC / barcode

Scan live with the camera, take a photo, or type the digits. Washtag checks the check digit on the phone, looks the product up, and shows how to use it with dosing notes and where to buy.
