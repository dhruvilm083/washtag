## Description (4000)

Washtag is a laundry planner. Tell it what's in the basket and it works out laundry day for you: which things wash together, which batch goes in first, when to move each load to the dryer, and which cycle to pick on your own machines. The washer and dryer stay busy, nothing sits in a wet pile, and you know when you'll be done before you start.

PLAN THE WHOLE BASKET
• Sorts everything into batches by care, colour and weight: towels with towels, darks with darks, wool on its own
• Sizes each batch to fit your drum, and splits anything too big
• Flags what should stay out of the machine, like dry-clean-only pieces

THE ORDER THAT FINISHES FIRST
• Picks the running order so the dryer is working while the next load washes
• Shows a finish time for the whole basket
• Tells you which items to pull out and hang up before the rest go in the dryer

BUILT FOR YOUR MACHINES
• Front loaders, top loaders and washer-dryer combos
• Look up your model for the maker's drum size, spin speed and the cycle names on your own control panel, or just pick the machine type
• Combo mode keeps every batch to what your combo can dry, runs air-dry loads first, and tells you when one Wash + Dry cycle will do

KNOWS YOUR CLOTHES
• Photograph a care label once and Washtag reads the symbols into plain Wash, Dry, Care and Do-not steps, with a confidence score on each
• Scan a barcode on detergent or clothing to look the product up
• Every plan follows the strictest label in the load, so nothing shrinks or runs

AND THE LITTLE THINGS
• Detergent amount for each load, HE or standard
• Household labels so you can see whose things are whose
• Product suggestions for the fabrics in the load, with the reason for each

Washtag keeps your saved items on your phone.
